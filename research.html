Master's research

Rainfall variability and drivers across Madagascar

IOGA, University of Antananarivo, July 2023 to July 2024

Supervisors: Prof. Rondrotiana Barimalala and Dr. Hoby Razafindrakoto.

  • Used CHIRPS satellite rainfall, ERA5 reanalysis, HadISST sea surface temperature and GEBCO topography with Python and CDO to define five climatic zones (Southwest, East, Central, West, North).
  • Defined wet and dry seasons for each region and measured variability with standardized anomalies, linear trends and Pearson correlation. Found a significant decline in precipitation.
  • Studied how ENSO, the Indian Ocean Dipole, the Indian Ocean Subtropical Dipole, the Mascarene High and the Mozambique Channel Trough influence extreme wet and dry years.

Machine learning for climate

Climate Change AI Virtual Summer School projects

July to August 2026

  • Flood mapping: a PyTorch CNN mapping surface water from Sentinel-1 radar images with Sentinel-2 proxy labels, evaluated with IoU, F1, precision and recall, and a note on proxy-label bias.
  • Statistical downscaling: a convolutional network (DeepESD) downscaling daily precipitation, with extremes (P98, RX1day) evaluated beyond RMSE and tested across two climate models.

About

I hold an M.Sc. in Earth Sciences (very good distinction) from the Institute and Observatory of Geophysics of Antananarivo (IOGA), University of Antananarivo. My thesis analysed how rainfall varies from year to year across Madagascar's climatic zones and which large-scale climate modes drive it.

I am now a trainee researcher in the ALBATROSS International Mentoring Programme, working on sub-seasonal rainfall prediction for early warning in south-west Madagascar. I also teach and speak about climate science to students and professionals.

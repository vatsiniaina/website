About

I hold an M.Sc. in Earth Sciences (very good distinction) from the Institute and Observatory of Geophysics of Antananarivo (IOGA), University of Antananarivo. My thesis analysed how rainfall varies from year to year across Madagascar's climatic zones and what drives it.

I am now a trainee researcher in the ALBATROSS International Mentoring Programme, working on sub-seasonal rainfall prediction for early warning in south-west Madagascar. I also teach and speak about climate science to students and professionals.

I work in Malagasy, French and English.

Research interests

Mesoscale convective systems and coastal convection

Their organization, their contribution to rainfall and their variability across timescales, with a focus on the Southwest Indian Ocean and Madagascar.

Tropical cyclones and rainfall impacts

How cyclones and their environments shape rainfall extremes and regional climate risk.

Rainfall variability and prediction with statistics and AI

Observational analysis of interannual to sub-seasonal variability using satellite and reanalysis data, and machine learning to improve forecasts and early-warning information.

Master's research: rainfall variability and drivers across Madagascar

IOGA, University of Antananarivo, July 2023 to July 2024

Supervisors: Prof. Rondrotiana Barimalala and Dr. Hoby Razafindrakoto.

  • Used CHIRPS, ERA5, HadISST and GEBCO data with Python and CDO to define five climatic zones (Southwest, East, Central, West, North).
  • Defined wet and dry seasons for each region and measured variability with standardized anomalies, trends and correlations. Found a significant decline in precipitation.
  • Studied how ENSO, the Indian Ocean Dipole, the Indian Ocean Subtropical Dipole, the Mascarene High and the Mozambique Channel Trough influence extreme wet and dry years.

Machine learning for climate: projects

Climate Change AI Virtual Summer School, July to August 2026

  • Flood mapping: a PyTorch CNN mapping surface water from Sentinel-1 radar images, checked with IoU, F1, precision and recall.
  • Downscaling: a convolutional network (DeepESD) that downscales daily precipitation, with extremes (P98, RX1day) evaluated beyond RMSE.
  • Streamflow: LSTM models on the CAMELS dataset, tested on an unseen basin.
